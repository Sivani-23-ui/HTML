🕉 About Lord Ganesha 🕉

🐖 Lord Ganesha is the elephant-headed Hindu deity known as the remover of obstacles and the god of wisdom, prosperity, and good fortune.
📖 He is worshipped at the beginning of all new ventures for success and blessings.
🎉 His festival, Ganesh Chaturthi, is celebrated with devotion and joy across India.
